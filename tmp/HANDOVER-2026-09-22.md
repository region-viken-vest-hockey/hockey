## A mistake made and corrected this session

Ran `season release-protection --request-id "operator:global-date-exclusions:2026-2027-repair"`
intending to release **one** protection (rvv-0028's start_time pin) but that
flag releases the **entire batch** under that request id — released 26 at
once, 25 unrelated. Caught it, confirmed the records were recoverable
(status flipped to `released`, not deleted) via your other session's git
history (`HEAD` at the time), and restored all 25 to `active` by directly
editing `status`/removing the release-metadata fields on those exact 25
IDs (kept the 1 intentional release). Verified hard verification still
passes clean afterward. **Lesson: use `--protection-id` for single releases,
never `--request-id` unless you mean the whole batch.**
